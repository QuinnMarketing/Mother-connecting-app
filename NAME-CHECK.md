# "Mummit" — Name Conflict Check

_Preliminary desk check, 7 Nov 2026. Not legal advice; confirm with a trade mark attorney before filing or launching._

## Summary

| Area | Finding | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Similar community name | **r/Mommit** — Reddit parenting community for mothers, ~2.7–2.8M members, covering medical care, sleep, feeding, etc. "Mummit" reads as the AU/UK spelling of the same name, in the same space. | **High** (confusion / implied Reddit affiliation) |
| Exact-name apps | No "Mummit" app found on the App Store / Google Play via web search. Nearest: "Mumit" (Spanish jewellery retailer, mumit.com) — different industry. | Low |
| Exact-name businesses | "Mummit" — a Finnish home care / everyday-help service (Facebook: mummitpalvelu). In Finnish *mummit* means "grannies". Different market. | Low (AU); Medium if expanding to the EU |
| Trade marks | No MUMMIT trade mark found in web-indexed results. Registers were **not** searched directly (see below). | Unknown — must check |
| Domains | Could not be checked from this environment (WHOIS/RDAP blocked). No live website found for mummit.com or mummit.com.au in search results. | Unknown — must check |
| Nearby marks | Mumsnet (UK parenting forum), Mum To Mum Ltd, MUM, MUMS Inc. — share the generic "mum" element, which is weak on its own. | Low–Medium |

## The main concern: r/Mommit

- Same audience (mothers), same function (peer Q&A, including health), and near-identical name — the only difference is the regional spelling of "Mom/Mum".
- Users may assume Mummit is the Australian version of r/Mommit or affiliated with Reddit. Reddit's "-it" naming pattern is well known.
- Brand risk: the app's promise is "no hostility, no judgement", which is the opposite of the reputation many people associate with Reddit-style forums.
- A subreddit name is not itself a registered trade mark, so a legal block is not certain, but passing-off / misleading-conduct arguments (Australian Consumer Law) and app store "impersonation" reviews are possible. Get an attorney's view.

2. **WIPO Global Brand Database** (international): https://branddb.wipo.int
3. **USPTO** (if US expansion): https://tmsearch.uspto.gov
4. **UK IPO** (UK expansion): https://trademarks.ipo.gov.uk
5. **ASIC business names / ABN Lookup**: https://abr.business.gov.au
6. **Domains**: mummit.com, mummit.com.au, mummit.au, mummit.app — check with any registrar (e.g. VentraIP, Crazy Domains, Namecheap). A .com.au / .au needs an ABN.
7. **App stores**: search "Mummit" directly in the App Store and Google Play.
8. **Social handles**: @mummit on Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X.

## Recommendation

Do not commit spend (logo, domain, marketing) to "Mummit" until a trade mark attorney has looked at the r/Mommit similarity. Options:
- **Proceed** if the register and domain checks are clear and the attorney is comfortable. File an AU trade mark early (Classes 9, 38, 42, 45).
- **Adjust** to a name that keeps the warm "mum" feel without echoing r/Mommit.
